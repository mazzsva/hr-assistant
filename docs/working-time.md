# Working Time and Shift Policy

## Standard hours

The standard week is 38 hours for production roles and 40 hours for salaried
roles. Working time is recorded in the time system on entry and exit; manual
corrections need manager approval within five working days.

## Shift patterns

Production runs three shifts: early (06:00-14:00), late (14:00-22:00), and night
(22:00-06:00). Shift plans are published at least three weeks in advance. A
published shift may be changed with less than 96 hours' notice only with the
employee's consent.

## Shift premiums

Night shift hours carry a 25% premium. Sunday hours carry a 50% premium. Public
holiday hours carry a 100% premium. Premiums are not combined; the highest single
applicable premium applies.

## Overtime

Overtime is voluntary and must be approved in advance. Approved overtime is
credited to a working time account, not paid out by default. Balances above 60
hours are paid out at the end of the following quarter.

Daily working time may not exceed 10 hours. The rest period between two shifts is
at least 11 hours.

## Remote work

Salaried roles that do not require plant presence may work remotely up to two days
per week with manager agreement. Production, maintenance, quality, and logistics
roles are on-site roles and are not eligible for remote work.

Remote work from outside Germany is limited to 20 working days per calendar year
and requires People team approval for tax and social security reasons.
