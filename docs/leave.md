# Leave and Absence Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave
accrues at 2.5 days per completed month of service. Employees who join partway
through a year receive a pro-rated entitlement.

Up to 10 unused days carry over into the next calendar year and must be taken by
31 March. Days not taken by that date are forfeited without payment.

## Requesting leave

Leave requests go through the shift planning system at least 14 calendar days
before the first day of absence. Requests covering more than 10 consecutive
working days need approval from the department lead as well as the direct manager.

During a production ramp the plant director may declare a blackout period of up to
four weeks in which only pre-approved and statutory leave is granted.

## Sick leave

Employees notify their manager before the start of their shift on the first day of
illness. A medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive calendar day
of illness, and from the first day if the absence follows or precedes annual leave.

Sick days do not count against annual leave and do not carry a waiting period.
Continued pay follows German statutory rules for the first six weeks of a single
illness.

## Parental leave

Parental leave follows the statutory entitlement. Employees notify the People team
at least seven weeks before the intended start date, and at least 13 weeks before
if the leave starts after the child's third birthday.

The company tops up parental allowance to 100% of base pay for the first eight
weeks for employees with more than 12 months of service.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to three months may be granted at the company's discretion after
12 months of service. Benefits other than statutory health insurance are suspended
during unpaid leave.
