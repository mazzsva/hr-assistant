# Hiring and Referral Policy

## Internal applications

Employees may apply for an internal opening after 12 months in their current role.
The current manager is informed once the employee reaches the second interview
stage, not before.

An internal move takes effect at the start of the month after a four-week handover,
unless both managers agree to a shorter handover.

## Referrals

Employees may refer external candidates for any open role. A referral bonus of
EUR 1,500 gross is paid for production and logistics roles and EUR 3,000 gross for
engineering and specialist roles.

The bonus is paid after the referred employee completes six months of service. Both
the referrer and the referred employee must be employed on the payment date. People
team members and hiring managers for the role in question are not eligible.

A candidate counts as referred only if the referral is logged before the candidate
applies. Referrals for candidates already in the applicant tracking system do not
qualify.

## Probation

New employees serve a six-month probation period with a notice period of two weeks
on either side. Probation may be shortened by the People team but not extended.

## Interviews

Every interview loop includes at least one interviewer from outside the hiring
team. Interviewers record structured feedback in the applicant tracking system
within 24 hours of the interview and before reading other interviewers' feedback.
